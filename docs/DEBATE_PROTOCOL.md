# Asynchronous policy debate protocol

Normative foundation for the neutral engine. Consult `docs/DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.md` for requirements, open decisions, and acceptance gates.

## Two simultaneous views

**The complete public record** includes every published candidate contribution, objections, assessments, and source documents. A candidate who created a debate cannot delete or privately suppress a contender's publication.

**The qualifying debate sequence** includes only complete reciprocal exchanges. A public response is not automatically a qualifying video.

## Opening

A candidate for an office publishes a video introducing a policy position, supporting materials, one to three specific questions, and a disclosed set of reciprocal time limits. Rules are versioned and cannot be changed retroactively.

## Reciprocal branch

Other verified candidates for the same office/election reply on independent branches. Each response is a full, intact video, maps answers to question timestamps, and may pose specific reciprocal questions.

A asks Q1–Q2 in A0. B answers and asks Q3–Q4 in B1. A answers Q3–Q4 in A2. The branch's first exchange qualifies only if A accepts B's answers to Q1–Q2 and B accepts A's answers to Q3–Q4.

**Unreviewed** is different from **unanswered**. **B's public video exists** whether the exchange qualifies or not. A disputed decision appears as a candidly attributed dispute, not a factual verdict by the engine. A footnote links to the complete B video and associated reasons.

New questions posed in A2 start the next exchange and do not reopen the already completed exchange. Full-record pages remain accessible even if any future exchange fails or is abandoned.

## Consent and original media

Original video bytes are retained. Technical full-length renditions are distinguished from edited derivatives. A contributor's permission for archival publication does not grant editing rights. Every derivative records its source IDs and edits; joint summaries require both candidates' precise approval of the same final artifact version.

## Resolution

Either candidate may propose a versioned synthesized policy. Acceptance by both of the **same exact version**, including attachments, establishes a jointly agreed policy. The platform does not objectively certify it as best. Partial agreement, later withdrawal, and remaining disagreement remain visible.

When agreed, a concise master plus progressively shorter faithful media versions may be authored, reviewed, and disseminated only with applicable permissions. The full original record survives.

## Explicitly pending

The user's interrupted sentence beginning `When a candidate uploads a video, whether the original video or ...` has not been completed. Do not invent the remaining rule.

## Security and governance

The candidate originator's power to assess questions never includes rights to delete others' records, impersonate approvals, operate platform-level restrictions, or edit others' media. Independent legal/security restrictions require a transparent process and are out of ordinary candidate roles.
