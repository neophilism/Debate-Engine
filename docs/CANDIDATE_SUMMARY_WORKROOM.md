# Candidate summary workroom

For each public two-party debate branch, /workspace/media/{branchId} is the authenticated editing/consent/approval workspace. The originator and contender each use their own verified candidacy identity. A plan contains selected original-video clips and the intended output time format. An original owner must affirm the exact in/out range of their own source video before rendering may be queued.

The workroom shows the artifact's draft manifest digest separately from the hash of the actual FFmpeg-rendered bytes. Both candidates must review and approve the same rendered video. A preview link is short-lived and authenticated. Final release remains blocked unless the two parties also accepted the same policy version. Full unedited source videos and all nonqualifying responses remain publicly available.

This workroom does not mean the video worker is deployed. It also does not automatically email candidates or disseminate to external channels. A privately credentialed isolated media worker and real multi-user browser acceptance are still required to operate production summaries.
